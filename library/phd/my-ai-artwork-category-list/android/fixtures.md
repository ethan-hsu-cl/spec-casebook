# My AI Artwork: All categories (Android) — what the phone needs before a run

What a run needs on the phone or the account, because none of it can be committed. Check it before the first segment;
a new row that needs a new photo or account state adds it here in the same draft.

## Phone, app and account
- Phone: Samsung Galaxy S23 (SM-S9110, adb serial RFCW2198L7B, Android 16, 1080 × 2340), on USB, Stay awake on, no
  PIN. Always pass the serial.
- PhotoDirector for Android 20.19.0 (90201900) or the TR's build; the Launcher shows the Test Server labels.
- My AI Artwork: Launcher › 「AI Photos」 › 「My Artwork」 (top right). The ☰ is `all_categories_btn` (content-desc
  「All categories」); the sheet's ✕ is 「Close」.
- Accounts (the user signs in; the agent never types a password):
  - **Premium** account with works (signed in by the user, 2026-11-11): every row except TC-025.
  - **Free** account, any works: TC-025 (run on 2026-11-11 with the phone's free, signed-out-of-Premium state).

## Works on the account
| Need | Used by |
| --- | --- |
| Works under at least three features, among them 「AI Art」 and 「AI Creative Studio」 | most rows |
| Recent holds photos and videos | TC-005 |
| At least one feature with no works (an empty tab) | TC-004, TC-009, TC-016 |
| Two or more works on 「AI Creative Studio」 (multi-select) | TC-019, TC-032 |
| A feature with a History button and works under it | TC-027 |

## Not available
- An analytics event log (TC-028 – TC-031, parked in phases.json).
